How Alignment Is Measured
CineSparq evaluates alignment across consistent signals so creators can see how projects align with real opportunities.
Back to Roadmap
ALIGNMENT CATEGORIES

Financial Alignment

How clearly a project’s financial structure and use of capital are communicated.

  • Budget clarity and realism
  • Visibility into how funds are allocated
  • Cash-flow logic (timing of funds in and out)

Legal Alignment

How clearly project rights and ownership are established.

  • Chain of title clarity
  • Rights ownership and control
  • Readiness to receive external funding

Package Alignment

How cohesively a project communicates its creative and production intent.

  • Consistency across materials
  • Clarity of creative vision
  • Team and scope articulation

Market Alignment

How clearly a project situates itself within an audience, cultural, or mandate context.

  • Audience definition
  • Comparable projects or references
  • Cultural, regional, or thematic relevance

Timeline Alignment

How clearly a project’s production timing and readiness are communicated.

  • Production window clarity
  • Stage of development or production
  • Compatibility with opportunity timelines

Collateral Alignment

Evaluated when relevant to debt-based opportunities.

  • Incentives or rebates
  • Pre-sales or guarantees
  • Other assignable elements
